# 💫 About Me

CS senior at the University of Connecticut, concentrating in Computational Data Analytics with a math minor.

I work on **agentic AI systems, systems design and gaming**.

- 🔬 Undergraduate researcher at the **NSF WISER IUCRC**, building a multi-agent platform for natural-language analytics over storm-impact data — SQL generation, FAISS retrieval, and ranking agents behind a routing layer, with ground-truth eval suites and read-only enforcement on model-generated queries.
- 🏆 **1st place, AI & Creativity track — HackUConn 2026** for an AI content protection tool using adversarial perturbations against diffusion-model image encoders.
- ⚡ Also enjoy systems work in C++ — most recently a BGP routing simulator over real CAIDA topology data, propagating 2.96M routes across 78,370 autonomous systems in ~17 seconds.
- 📫 Open to summer 2027 internships in AI/ML engineering and backend infrastructure.
